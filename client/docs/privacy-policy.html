Privacy Policy

FyBud Customer Engagement Platform (CEP)
Last updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FyBud (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Customer Engagement Platform and related messaging integrations (WhatsApp, Instagram, Email/Gmail).

1. Information we collect

  • Account information you provide (such as admin username and authentication credentials for our dashboard).
  • Customer communication data processed on your behalf, including message content, sender identifiers (phone numbers, Instagram IDs, email addresses), and related metadata needed to deliver inbox functionality.
  • Technical logs (request timestamps, error diagnostics) used to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use information

  • To provide omnichannel inbox, reply, and customer-context features.
  • To connect to messaging providers you authorize (Meta, Google).
  • To maintain security, troubleshooting, and service reliability.

3. Sharing

We process messages through the third-party platforms you connect (for example Meta WhatsApp/Instagram APIs and Google Gmail APIs). We do not sell personal information. Service providers that host infrastructure may process data solely to run the service.

4. Data retention

Message and contact data are retained for as long as needed to provide the service to the business customer, or until deleted by that customer / administrator, subject to legal obligations.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate for a development and production messaging workspace. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Your choices

Business administrators may disconnect channel integrations and request deletion of workspace data by contacting us. End customers should contact the business they messaged for support regarding their conversations.

7. Children’s privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
